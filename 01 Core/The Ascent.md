# The Ascent

The Guide describes the terrain and its visible danger. The team chooses a route and names one climber to lead. If an outcome is safe and certain, it happens without a check. Ask for a check only when failure would change the climb.

## Tile checks

Use one STEPS platform and shared pieces. Before a check, the Guide states the reward, the fall risk, and the tile count:

| Tiles | Crossing |
| ---: | --- |
| 1 | Precarious but prepared |
| 2 | Dangerous; the ordinary climb |
| 3 | Severe weather or exposed terrain |
| 4 | Desperate; several dangers at once |

The leader flips or tosses the required pieces onto the platform, one at a time, with a visible turn in the air. The team chooses each available piece. A teammate may throw for the leader; everyone may advise. Do not touch the platform or placed pieces during a check. If **any piece falls off**, including an earlier one, or a throw misses, the check fails. If all pieces remain, it succeeds. Leave them in place.

The leader's unused trained skill reduces the check by one tile if it addresses the danger; mark it used until camp. Another climber may first make a supporting check, such as placing a piton. Success reduces the main check by one if it removes a danger; failure has the usual consequences. The Guide decides what fits. A sound route or equipment may remove a danger without a check. Allow only one reduction from support or preparation, plus one from skill, never below one tile. Storm, failing light, a leader at 2 Exposure, each climber at 3 Exposure, and Rope Strain at 2 or 3 may each add one. A result over four needs changed circumstances.

The structure persists between checks and scenes. Clear it only after securing a new camp or a true fall.

If no pieces remain to throw, the overloaded structure sheds a piece. Resolve a failed check normally.

## Slips, Strain, and falls

Track **Rope Strain** for the whole team from 0 to 3. If Strain is below 3, a fallen tile means a **slip**: the check fails, the leader gains 1 Exposure, and Rope Strain rises by 1. The rope catches them; the team does **not** fall.

The attempted action does not succeed. The Guide shows one concrete setback already implied by the danger, such as lost time, damaged equipment, worse footing, or a blocked route. Leave any discoveries intact. Return fallen tiles to the supply after resolving the check; pieces still on the platform stay in place.

At 2 or 3 Strain, add one tile to subsequent checks. If a check fails while the rope is **already at 3 Strain**, the catch finally fails **instead of an ordinary slip**: the entire tethered team falls to its last secured camp or anchor. Each climber gains 1 Exposure, the attempted route is lost, Rope Strain returns to 0, and the platform is cleared. The leader does not take an extra point for slipping. Describe the cascade: one foot slips, the line snaps taut, and every climber is dragged after the first. The Guide may add a specific cost already signaled by the danger, but not a surprise death.

Strain does not disappear after an ordinary success. The Guide may offer a secure anchor as a route objective; reaching it reduces Strain by 1 and gives the team a nearer place to land after a true fall. An anchor does not clear the platform. A new camp clears all Strain. The team cannot declare an anchor in empty air or repeat a safe action to erase Strain.

At **2 Exposure**, a climber adds one tile to checks they lead. At **3 Exposure**, they add one tile to every team check, including checks led by others. They can still lead or throw. Exposure stays at 3 if they would gain more. Share the lead before anyone reaches this point. Death requires an established danger that makes rescue impossible, never a bare tile result.

## Making camp

> ![[Art/Portaledge.webp]]

To secure a new camp, make one final check against its exposed ground, weather, or shelter work. Name the danger and tile count as usual. On failure, resolve the slip or fall; the camp is not yet secure and no Exposure clears. The team must change its approach before trying again.

On success, clear the platform and Rope Strain. Every climber clears 1 Exposure and refreshes their trained skill. A full week of rest clears all Exposure, but the Guide changes the mountain. The team decides whether the time is worth it.

## The climb's rhythm

Each leg has a destination camp and two or three meaningful obstacles. The team scouts, chooses a route, lives with falls, and secures or abandons the camp. Each secured new camp counts as one **ascent** and roughly one week. A full week spent resting at camp also passes. In either case, record the changes from [[The Climbers]]. The Guide does not advance weeks just to punish caution.

When completed ascents equal twice the number of climbers currently on the rope, end the game. The summit remains out of reach, but the team gains a view none imagined. Ask each climber what they now know about the others and whether they would keep climbing together.
