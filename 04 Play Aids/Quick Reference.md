# Quick Reference

## Before a check

1. Describe the danger and ask for the team's route.
2. Name the leader, the reward, the slip setback, and the last secured anchor.
3. Set 1 to 4 tiles. The leader's unused trained skill may reduce the count by one. Successful support or specific preparation may reduce it by one more, never below one. Storm, failing light, a leader at 2 Exposure, each climber at 3 Exposure, or Rope Strain at 2 or 3 may each add one. A result over four needs changed circumstances.

## At the platform

- Use the same shared structure until a new camp is secured.
- Flip or toss one chosen piece at a time with a visible turn in the air.
- Teammates may take throws or advise.
- If any piece falls off, stop: the check fails and the rope catches a slip, unless Strain was already 3.
- If all required pieces remain, the action succeeds. Leave them in place.

## After a failed check

- On an ordinary slip, the leader gains 1 Exposure; Rope Strain rises by 1 (maximum 3).
- The action fails. Show a concrete setback and what the slip taught.
- At Strain 2 or 3, add one tile to future checks.
- If Strain was already 3 **before** failure, the entire team falls to its last anchor instead. Each gains 1 Exposure (no extra point for the leader); clear Strain and the platform.
- At 2 Exposure, add one tile to checks that climber leads.
- At 3 Exposure, that climber adds one tile to every team check, but can still lead or throw. Exposure cannot exceed 3.

## Securing camp

- Make one final check for the camp. On failure, resolve the slip or fall; no Exposure clears. Change the approach before another attempt.
- On success, clear the platform and recover all pieces.
- Clear Rope Strain.
- Each climber clears 1 Exposure. A full week of rest clears all, but the mountain changes.
- Refresh each trained skill's once-per-leg use.
- After each new camp or full week of rest, increase WEEKS by 1 and record one permanent bodily change.
- Count one ascent for each secured new camp. End after twice as many ascents as climbers currently on the rope.
