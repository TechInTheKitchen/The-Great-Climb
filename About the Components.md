# About the Components

The Great Climb uses a [STEPS Starter Pack](https://www.stepsgames.com/products/steps-starter-pack-1-4-players-32-steps-tiles) as its physical resolution tool. Its shared structure, checks, rope, Exposure, and setting are original adaptations, not STEPS rules. This independent game is not affiliated with or endorsed by STEPS Games.

The [official STEPS rules](https://www.stepsgames.com/blogs/news/rules) explain the original game. For this game, follow [[The Ascent]] where procedures differ. Pieces are shared; each check specifies a number of throws; a fallen piece fails the check.

Bring one STEPS platform and its pieces, paper for the rope team and map, and a way to mark Exposure. A player may direct another person's throw.
